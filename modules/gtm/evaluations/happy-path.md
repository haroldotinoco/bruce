# GTM Module Evaluation: Happy Path

## Scenario: B2B SaaS Compliance Tool - Strategy → Launch → Optimization

### Week 1: Strategy Definition

**Input:** New venture from AddVenture module
- Product: AI compliance automation platform
- Target: Mid-market finance/healthcare companies ($50-500K revenue)
- Initial budget: $50K/month
- Timeline: Launch within 4 weeks

**GTM Strategy Workflow Execution:**

1. **Channel Analysis**
   - Evaluate: Paid search (LinkedIn ads, Google search), content marketing, email, partnerships, direct sales
   - Ranking: Paid search #1 (high-intent buyers), content #2 (brand/thought leadership), email #3 (nurture)
   - Fit assessment: Excellent for B2B, product is compliance-focused (strong in regulatory blogs/groups)

2. **Channel Selection**
   - Selected: Paid search, content marketing, email, partnerships
   - Budget allocation: Paid search $25K, content/organic $15K, email $5K, partnerships $5K
   - Launch sequence: Paid search week 1, content calendar by week 2, partnerships by week 3

3. **Content System**
   - Messaging: "Cut compliance overhead by 80%, stay audit-ready"
   - Channels:
     - Blog: Regulatory updates, compliance best practices (3x/week)
     - Paid: Product demo videos, ROI case studies (daily)
     - Email: Nurture sequence on compliance pain points (2x/week)
   - Calendar: 12-week content roadmap created

**Output:** Strategy approved, $50K monthly budget allocated, content calendar live

### Week 2-3: Campaign Launch

**Campaign 1: Paid Search (LinkedIn Ads)**
- Budget: $25K/month
- Targeting: CFOs, compliance managers at $50-500M revenue companies
- Creative: "Reduce compliance hours by 80%" + ROI calculator CTA
- A/B test: Headline variants (4), audience segments (3)
- Expected CAC: $400

**Campaign 2: Content Marketing**
- Blog launches: "Regulatory Compliance 2024 Update" (1,000+ words, SEO-optimized)
- Projected organic reach: 500 visitors/month by week 8
- Email capture: 10% of visitors
- Expected CAC: $150

**Campaign 3: Email Nurture**
- Sequence: 5-email series on compliance pain point
- Targeting: Existing contacts + blog visitors
- Expected CAC: $75 (lowest because warm audience)

### Week 4: Performance Monitoring

**Actual Results (After 2 Weeks):**

**Paid Search:**
- Impressions: 4,200
- Clicks: 89
- CTR: 2.1%
- Signups: 8
- CAC: $3,125 (5.8x target! Issue detected)

**Analysis:** CAC too high - likely audience too broad or creative not resonating

**Action:** Pause broad targeting, focus on "compliance audit failure risk" keywords, refine messaging

**Week 4 Results (After adjustments):**

**Paid Search (Optimized):**
- Impressions: 2,100 (less volume)
- Clicks: 67
- CTR: 3.2%
- Signups: 12
- CAC: $1,667 (still 4.2x target)

**Analysis:** Better CTR but CAC still high - signal: audience may not be ready to buy

**Decision:** Reallocate paid search budget

### Week 5-8: Rebalancing

**Trigger:** Paid search CAC > 1.5x target for 2+ weeks

**Rebalancing Decision:**
- Reduce paid search: $25K → $12K (pause unprofitable audience)
- Increase content marketing: $15K → $20K (invest in organic lead generation)
- Maintain email: $5K (good performer)
- Add partnerships: $13K (partner with accounting software companies for co-marketing)

**Rationale:** Content marketing CAC is $150 (on target), partnerships offer co-marketing (lower CAC), paid search needs more time to optimize

### Week 8-12: Optimization

**Content Marketing Results:**
- Blog traffic: 1,200 visitors/month
- Email capture rate: 12%
- Email list growth: 144 new contacts
- Cost per lead: $150 (on target)
- Activation rate (free trial): 35%
- CAC: $429 (1.1x target!)

**Partnership Results:**
- Co-marketing campaign with accounting software partner
- Leads generated: 25
- CAC: $520 (1.3x target)
- Activation rate: 40%

**Email Results:**
- Open rate: 28%
- Click rate: 4.2%
- Free trial signups: 18
- CAC: $278 (0.7x target - best performer!)

**Paid Search (Optimized Version):**
- After targeting refinement and creative iteration
- CAC: $380 (0.95x target - nearly on target after optimization)
- Volume: 6 signups/week

### Week 12: Monthly Review & Governance

**Monthly Traction Report:**
- Total signups: 85
- Total spend: $50K
- Overall CAC: $588 (1.47x target, slightly high)
- Activated users: 32 (38% activation rate)
- Average LTV estimate: $18K (based on product pricing)
- LTV/CAC ratio: 30.6x (excellent!)

**Channel Breakdown:**
| Channel | Spend | Signups | CAC | Performance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email | $5K | 18 | $278 | Excellent |
| Content | $20K | 34 | $588 | Good |
| Paid Search | $12K | 6 | $2,000 | Poor |
| Partnerships | $13K | 25 | $520 | Good |
| **Total** | **$50K** | **85** | **$588** | **On track** |

**Governance Decision:**
- **Email:** Continue (best performer)
- **Content:** Scale (allocate more budget, hire second writer)
- **Partnerships:** Scale (pursue additional co-marketing partners)
- **Paid search:** Pause/pivot (not achieving target despite optimization)
- **New experiment:** Influencer outreach in compliance space ($3-5K test budget)

**Budget Reallocation for Month 2:**
- Email: $5K → $8K (+$3K)
- Content: $20K → $28K (+$8K, hire contractor)
- Partnerships: $13K → $12K (-$1K, established pipeline)
- Paid search: $12K → $0 (pause, reallocate)
- Influencer test: $0 → $2K (new test)
- **Total: Still $50K**

**Report to Startup Ops:**
- Monthly signups: 85 (on track for 300+/month if maintained)
- Monthly revenue impact: $0 (conversion and payment takes time)
- Activation rate improving (38% → target 50%+)
- LTV/CAC ratio excellent (30x)
- Recommendation: Scale content + partnerships, pause paid search, test influencer
- Next review: 1 week

### Week 13-16: Scaling

**Content Marketing Scaling:**
- Hired second writer
- Output: 6 blog posts/week (vs 3)
- Results: 2,400 visitors/month, 288 new email contacts
- CAC trend: $565 (slightly higher due to new writer ramp, but scale improving)

**Partnership Expansion:**
- Established 3 new partnerships (accounting software, HR platforms, finance tools)
- Co-marketing campaigns live with 2 partners
- Monthly leads from partnerships: 35-50
- CAC: $450 (improving as partnerships mature)

**Email Performance:**
- List size: 450+ contacts
- Monthly signups from email: 22-25
- CAC: $320-360 (still best performer)
- Email engagement: Open rate 31%, click rate 5.1%

**Influencer Experiment Results:**
- Budget: $2K for 2 targeted compliance influencers
- Reach: 15K followers
- Signups: 3
- CAC: $667 (above target, not scaling)
- Decision: Kill experiment, focus on earned influencer coverage through content

### Month 2 Results

**Total Signups:** 92 (87 from organic/partnerships, 5 from paid)
**Total Spend:** $52K
**Overall CAC:** $565 (slightly above target due to content scaling, but improving)
**Activated Users:** 36 (39% activation rate)

**Key Success Metrics:**
- ✓ CAC trending toward target (from $588 → $565)
- ✓ Email channel outperforming (best CAC, best activation)
- ✓ Content marketing on target
- ✓ Partnerships delivering solid volume
- ✓ LTV/CAC ratio >25x (excellent)
- ✓ Activation rate improving (targeting 45%+)

### Expected 3-Month Trend

**Month 3 Projection (with current trajectory):**
- Monthly signups: 110-120
- CAC: $480 (closer to target)
- Activated users: 50-55
- LTV/CAC ratio: 30+x (maintained)
- Product-market fit confidence: High

**Success Criteria Met:**
✓ Channel strategy successful (content + partnerships + email working)
✓ Budget discipline maintained (no overspending)
✓ CAC approaching target (optimization path clear)
✓ Sustainable growth metrics (activation rate good)
✓ Scalable model (can increase budget without proportional CAC increase)

---

## Key Success Factors in Happy Path

1. **Fast learning loops:** Weekly review identified paid search problem by week 4
2. **Quick rebalancing:** Reallocated budget within 1 week of identifying underperformance
3. **Multi-channel approach:** Email + content + partnerships created balanced portfolio
4. **Clear thresholds:** Triggered rebalancing on 1.5x CAC rule (policy-driven)
5. **Experimentation:** Tested influencer, learned quickly, pivoted
6. **Scalable channels:** Content and partnerships scale without proportional cost increase
7. **Activation focus:** Tracked activation rate, not just signups (quality over volume)

This represents optimal GTM execution: strategy definition → rapid optimization → sustainable scaling.
