# GTM Budget Policy

## Budget Allocation Framework

### Core Allocation Rules

**Rule 1: Maximum Single Channel Cap**
- No channel may exceed 60% of total monthly budget
- Intent: Prevent over-reliance on single channel

**Rule 2: Minimum Channel Funding**
- Paid channels: $500/month minimum (organic/earned have no minimum)
- Intent: Ensure sufficient volume to measure and optimize

**Rule 3: Experimentation Budget**
- Reserve 10-15% of total budget for new channel testing and experiments
- Intent: Enable growth opportunities while protecting core channel performance

**Rule 4: Geographic/Segment Allocation**
- If targeting multiple geographies/segments, allocate budget proportional to TAM/priority
- Example: If 70% TAM in US and 30% in EU, allocate accordingly

### Budget Allocation Process

1. **Set total monthly budget** (based on funding and goals)
2. **Allocate across channels** following above rules
3. **Allocate within channels** (e.g., search budget split between keywords, paid social budget split between campaigns)
4. **Set daily/weekly budgets** to enable monitoring and adjustment
5. **Review weekly** for spending pace vs. plan
6. **Adjust monthly** based on performance

## Reallocation Triggers

### Automatic Reallocation Triggers

**Trigger 1: CAC Exceeds Target**
- When a channel's CAC > 1.5x target for 2 consecutive weeks:
  - Freeze new spend on that channel
  - Analyze why CAC increased (audience change? saturation? creative fatigue?)
  - Reallocate budget to channels performing near target

**Trigger 2: Volume Drop**
- When a channel delivers <80% of expected volume for 1 week:
  - Investigate cause (platform algorithm changes? budget insufficient?)
  - If cause unclear, freeze 20% of budget pending investigation
  - If cause is known, reallocate 25% to other channels

**Trigger 3: New High-Performer Emerges**
- When a new/secondary channel achieves CAC <0.8x target:
  - Flag for scaling
  - Reallocate up to 20% from underperforming channels to new performer
  - Gradual scaling (don't increase >50% in single week)

**Trigger 4: Single Channel Approaching 60% Cap**
- When any channel reaches 50% of budget:
  - Begin rebalancing immediately
  - Reduce spend on top channel by 10% per week until at 45% or below
  - Reallocate freed budget to underperforming channels

**Trigger 5: Quarterly Review**
- At the start of each quarter, conduct full rebalancing analysis regardless of performance
- May reallocate up to 20% of budget based on new priorities/learnings

### Manual Reallocation Triggers

**Portfolio leadership may trigger reallocation for:**
- Strategic shift (new priority market/segment)
- Competitive response (competitor aggressively entering channel)
- Resource constraints (team member departure affecting channel)
- Brand considerations (reputational concern on a platform)

**Requirement:** Written justification documenting business rationale.

## Experiment Budget Management

### Experiment Budget Rules

**Total Reserve:** 10-15% of monthly budget designated for experiments

**Sub-allocation:**
- New channel testing: Max 50% of experiment budget (5-8% of total)
- Creative testing (A/B variants): Max 30% of experiment budget
- Audience testing: Max 20% of experiment budget

### New Channel Testing Process

**Phase 1: Validation (2-4 weeks, $1K-5K)**
- Test minimal viable approach on new channel
- Goal: Determine if channel can achieve target CAC
- Success criteria: CAC within 2x target, minimum 50 conversions

**Phase 2: Optimization (4-8 weeks, $5K-15K)**
- Scale winning approach from Phase 1
- Test creative variants, audience segments, messaging
- Goal: Get CAC to 1.2x target or better

**Phase 3: Scale Decision (week 8)**
- Review Phase 2 results
- Decision: Promote to core channel, maintain as test, or kill
- If promote: Allocate ongoing budget from core pool

**Phase 4: Sunset Failed Tests (if applicable)**
- Kill budget immediately if not meeting success criteria
- Extract learnings (why did it fail? channel misalignment? audience? creative?)
- Document for future reference

### Experiment Reporting

Every month, report on active experiments:
- Experiment name, channel, budget, duration
- Current CAC vs. target
- Volumes (impressions, clicks, conversions)
- Projected outcome (on-track, at-risk, failed)
- Recommendation (continue, pause, kill)

## Budget Adjustments & Rebalancing Process

### Monthly Rebalancing

Every month (first Friday):

1. **Review performance** by channel (CAC, volume, ROI)
2. **Identify triggers** (CAC >1.5x, volume <80%, new high-performer)
3. **Propose reallocation** (max ±20% per channel per month)
4. **Model impact** (where will reallocated budget go? expected CAC/volume?)
5. **Implement gradually** (spread over 2 weeks to avoid overshooting)
6. **Monitor** (first 2 weeks of new allocation)
7. **Adjust** (if rebalancing not achieving goal, fine-tune further)

### Gradual Adjustment Best Practice

**Don't:** Cut channel budget 50% in single week

**Do:** Cut budget 10-15% per week over 3-4 weeks

**Rationale:** Allows platform algorithms (paid channels) to reoptimize rather than sudden disruption

### Maximum Monthly Adjustment

| Scenario | Max Adjustment |
|---|---|
| High performer (CAC <0.8x target) | +50% per month |
| Underperformer (CAC >1.5x target) | -25% per month |
| Kill-threshold channel | Can go to $0 (pause spend) |
| Testing channel | Can vary 30% week-to-week |

## Emergency Budget Scenarios

### Opportunity Scaling (Positive Case)

**Scenario:** Product hits viral moment or major PR event; demand spike expected

**Action:**
- Can increase core channel budgets up to 100% above normal (if revenue supports)
- Experiment budget can be temporarily suspended and reallocated
- Should be time-limited (4-8 weeks maximum) until demand normalizes

**Approval:** Portfolio leadership approval required

### Emergency Pause / Freeze

**Scenario:** Major brand issue, platform policy violation, or market crisis

**Action:**
- Can immediately pause any/all channels
- Spend frozen while issue investigated
- Partial restart once issue resolved

**Approval:** Portfolio leadership approval (any level can escalate, leadership decides)

### Budget Cap / Spending Freeze

**Scenario:** Funding crisis, projected shortfall, or strategic pivot

**Action:**
- Reduce all channel budgets proportionally (maintain allocation ratios)
- Prioritize core channels (highest ROI)
- Suspend experiment budget entirely
- Extend timeline to profitability

**Timeline:** Announce within 24 hours, implement within 1 week

## Budget Monitoring & Pacing

### Weekly Spend Pace Check

Every Monday morning, verify:
1. Actual spend vs. planned spend (should be within 10%)
2. Any channels significantly overspending (may indicate platform issue)
3. Any channels underspending (may indicate budget limit hit or quality score issues)
4. Forecast for full month (on-track, over-track, under-track?)

### Daily Spending Limits

For paid channels, set daily budget limits:
- Formula: `Monthly budget / 30 * 1.1` (10% buffer for daily variance)
- Example: $3,000/month = $100/day budget limit
- Prevents single day from consuming too much budget

### Spend Forecasting

Every Friday, forecast:
- Current week actual spend
- Projected full month spend (based on pace)
- Variance from plan (over/under budget)
- Action items (pause channels if overspend projected, accelerate if underspend)

## Channel Cost Management

### Cost-Per-Acquisition Targets by Channel

| Channel | B2B SaaS Target | B2C Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid Search | $300-600 | $20-40 | Most efficient, limited scale |
| Paid Social | $150-400 | $15-35 | Good scale, higher variability |
| Content Marketing | $50-150 | $10-20 | Highest leverage long-term |
| Email | $25-75 | $5-15 | Best retention channel |
| Organic Search | $0-50 | $0-10 | Long payoff period |
| Partnerships | $100-300 | $20-50 | Co-marketing mutual benefit |
| Direct Sales | $2,000-5,000 | N/A | Enterprise/high-touch only |

### Cost Reduction Opportunities

**Q1-Q2 (Every quarter):**
1. Audit top 10 expense items (highest-spend sources)
2. Negotiate with vendors/platforms (media buy discounts, rate reductions)
3. Test automation (e.g., dynamic creative, automated bidding)
4. Identify and eliminate low-ROI spend (bottom 10% performers)
5. Target: 10-15% cost reduction without volume loss

## Capital Allocation by Business Model

### B2B SaaS (Multi-year LTV Model)

**Recommended Allocation:**
- Paid (search, social, ads): 40-50%
- Content & organic: 20-30%
- Email & retention: 10-15%
- Direct sales (if enterprise): 10-20%

**Rationale:** LTV justifies higher early CAC since customer value accrues over years

### B2C SaaS / Freemium

**Recommended Allocation:**
- Paid (social, search): 50-60%
- Organic & virality: 20-30%
- Email/lifecycle: 10-15%

**Rationale:** Shorter payback requires efficient paid channels; virality critical

### Enterprise / High-Touch

**Recommended Allocation:**
- Direct sales & partnerships: 60-70%
- Content marketing: 20-30%
- Paid (selective, brand awareness): 0-10%

**Rationale:** Sales-driven model; large contract values justify high sales cost

## Experiment Budget Allocation Best Practices

### Balanced Portfolio Approach

Allocate experiment budget across categories:

**10% of experiment budget (1-1.5% of total):**
- 40% testing new channels
- 30% testing new audience segments (within existing channels)
- 20% testing creative/messaging variants
- 10% testing emerging platforms/tactics

**Example:** $100K monthly budget
- Experiment reserve: $10-15K
- New channel testing: $4-6K
- Audience testing: $3-4.5K
- Creative testing: $2-3K
- Emerging tactics: $1-1.5K

### Time-Boxing Experiments

All experiments must have:
- **Start date:** When does experiment begin?
- **End date:** Hard stop; no exceptions (gather data then decide)
- **Success criteria:** What does "success" look like? (CAC <X? Volume >Y?)
- **Decision date:** When will go/no-go decision be made?

**Example:** LinkedIn Ads test
- Start: January 1
- End: February 15 (6 weeks)
- Success criteria: CAC <$400, minimum 100 signups
- Decision date: February 17 (board report)

## Reporting & Transparency

### Monthly Budget Report

First Friday of each month, report:
1. **Spending by channel** (actual vs. budget, variance)
2. **Cost per acquisition** by channel (vs. target)
3. **Total volume** by channel (signups, activations, revenue)
4. **Budget reallocations** made (what changed? why?)
5. **Experiment status** (active, completed, killed)
6. **Forecast to profitability** (when will unit economics hit positive ROI?)

### Quarterly Budget Review

Every quarter:
1. **Budget vs. actual** (how accurately did we forecast?)
2. **Channel performance** (which channels beat/missed target CAC?)
3. **Experiment ROI** (which experiments justified their spend?)
4. **Process improvements** (what could we do better?)
5. **Next quarter forecast** (projected budget allocation, targets)

## Budget Policy Exceptions

### Exception Request Process

To override budget policy:

1. **Submit request** with business rationale
2. **Include data** supporting request (competitive threat, customer demand, market opportunity)
3. **Define scope** (how much? how long? what's the impact if wrong?)
4. **Get approval** from Portfolio Head + Leadership
5. **Document decision** with date, requester, business case, approval signature

### Common Exceptions

**Exception 1: Temporary >60% single channel**
- Business case: New channel showing exceptional CAC (0.5x target)
- Timeline: 4 weeks to scale, then return to 60% max
- Approval required

**Exception 2: Experiment budget >15% in specific month**
- Business case: Time-sensitive opportunity (platform launching, seasonal window)
- Timeline: Limited to 2-4 weeks
- Approval required

**Exception 3: Below-minimum spend on important channel**
- Business case: Strategic channel requiring presence despite CAC challenges
- Rationale: Brand positioning, PR opportunity, partnership importance
- Minimum: $250/month (below normal $500 minimum)
- Approval required, time-limited
