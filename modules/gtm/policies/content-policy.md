# GTM Content Policy

## Brand Voice & Compliance

### Brand Voice Guidelines

**Tone Dimensions:**

**Professionalism Level:** (1=Casual, 10=Formal)
- B2B Enterprise: 8-10 (professional, credible, data-driven)
- B2B SMB: 6-8 (professional but approachable)
- B2C SaaS: 4-6 (friendly, conversational, occasional humor)
- Consumer: 2-5 (casual, relatable, entertaining)

**Technical Depth:** (1=Layman, 10=Expert)
- Decision-maker focused: 4-6 (explain benefits, avoid jargon)
- Technical buyer focused: 8-9 (technical depth, implementation details)
- End-user focused: 2-4 (simple, benefit-driven)

**Emotional Appeal:** (1=Logical, 10=Emotional)
- B2B: 3-5 (emphasis on ROI, efficiency, risk reduction)
- Consumer: 5-8 (emotional triggers, aspirational, community)
- Healthcare: 6-8 (trust, safety, empathy)

### Voice Consistency Rules

1. **Maintain tone across all channels** — Same brand voice in paid ads, blog, email, social
2. **Avoid contradictory messaging** — Don't position as "simple" in one channel and "powerful" in another
3. **Consistent terminology** — Use same product terms/features names across all content
4. **Visual consistency** — Colors, fonts, imagery aligned across channels
5. **Founder voice** — If founder-first brand, maintain consistent personality/perspective

## Content Approval Requirements

### Approval Workflow

**All content must follow this path before publication:**

```
Content creation → Internal review → Brand compliance check → Legal/Privacy check (if needed) → Approval → Scheduling → Publication
```

### Content Types & Approval Authority

| Content Type | Created By | Review By | Approver | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blog post | Content team | Editor, brand | Content lead | 3-5 days |
| Ad copy | Content/channel team | Brand, performance | Campaign manager | 1-2 days |
| Email | Email specialist | Brand, compliance | Email lead | 1-2 days |
| Landing page | Product/content | Design, brand | Product/marketing lead | 5-7 days |
| Whitepaper | Content specialist | Editor, SME | Content/product lead | 10-14 days |
| Case study | Growth/content | Editor, legal | Head of GTM | 5-7 days |
| Social media post | Social manager | Brand | Social lead | 24 hours |
| Video script | Video producer | Brand, product | Video producer | 3-5 days |

### Approval Checklist

Before any content publishes, verify:
- [ ] Brand voice consistent with guidelines
- [ ] No factual claims without sources/data
- [ ] Legal/compliance review if including terms, pricing, claims
- [ ] No competitive disparagement (positive positioning vs. negative competitor focus)
- [ ] Product/feature accuracy reviewed by product team
- [ ] Tone appropriate for target audience
- [ ] Call-to-action clear and aligned with funnel stage
- [ ] Visual assets on-brand (colors, fonts, imagery)
- [ ] No deprecated product terminology or outdated features mentioned

## Publishing Cadence & Channel Strategy

### Blog Publishing

**Frequency:** 1-2 posts per week (for actively growing ventures)

**Cadence:**
- Major content (pillar/cornerstone): Weekly
- Secondary content (supporting): Bi-weekly
- Product updates/news: Ad-hoc

**SEO Requirements:**
- Target keyword in headline (H1)
- Target keyword in first paragraph
- Target keyword density 1-2%
- Internal linking (2-3 links to relevant content)
- Meta description <160 characters

### Email Publishing

**Frequency:** 1-3 emails per week depending on list health

**Cadence:**
- Nurture sequence: 2x per week
- Newsletter: 1x per week
- Product updates: As needed
- Promotional: 1x per month maximum (to avoid unsubscribe)

**Engagement Targets:**
- Open rate: >25% (B2B), >20% (B2C)
- Click rate: >3% (B2B), >2% (B2C)
- Unsubscribe rate: <0.5%

### Social Media Publishing

**Frequency:** 3-5x per week per platform

**Cadence:**
- Organic content: Daily
- Engagement (comments, conversations): Multiple times daily
- Promotional: 1-2x per week

**Platform-Specific Guidance:**
- **LinkedIn (B2B):** Educational, thought leadership, company updates
- **Twitter/X:** News, quick tips, real-time engagement, industry commentary
- **Instagram (B2C):** Visual storytelling, user-generated content, lifestyle
- **TikTok (B2C, consumer):** Trending audio, behind-the-scenes, personality-driven
- **Facebook:** Community-building, longer-form updates, targeted ads

### Paid Ad Cadence

**Ad Copy Rotation:**
- Refresh creative every 2 weeks (prevent ad fatigue)
- Minimum 3 headline variants per ad
- Minimum 3 body copy variants per ad
- Minimum 2-3 image/video variants

**Testing Cadence:**
- A/B test one variable per week (don't test too many variables simultaneously)
- Minimum 100 conversions per variant to reach statistical significance
- Run tests minimum 2 weeks before evaluating

## Prohibited Content

### Absolutely Prohibited (Never Publish)

- [ ] False or misleading claims about product/service
- [ ] Comparative claims without data supporting differentiation
- [ ] Fake testimonials or customer quotes
- [ ] Misrepresentation of competitor offerings
- [ ] Use of competitor logos/trademarks without permission
- [ ] Hate speech, discrimination, harmful stereotypes
- [ ] Copyright infringement or plagiarism
- [ ] Sharing confidential customer data without consent
- [ ] Unsubstantiated health claims (if health-related product)

### Generally Prohibited (Escalate for Exception)

- [ ] Negative competitor commentary (focus on own value, not attacking competition)
- [ ] Controversial political/religious content (unless brand explicitly stands on topic)
- [ ] Graphic/violent content (not appropriate for most B2B audiences)
- [ ] Content that could expose legal liability
- [ ] Outdated pricing or product information
- [ ] Unvetted third-party content (e.g., user submissions)

### Channel-Specific Prohibitions

**Email:**
- Click bait subject lines (e.g., "You won't believe...")
- Misleading "urgent" or "limited-time" claims
- Excessively long subject lines (>50 characters)

**Paid Ads:**
- Before/after claims without substantiation
- Claims of medical benefits (if not health-focused product)
- Misleading discount claims (e.g., "$100 off! *after signing 2-year contract")

**Social Media:**
- Excessive self-promotion (ratio >60% promotional content)
- Auto-DM spam or bot-like behavior
- Engagement-baiting ("Like if you agree!")

## Content Approval Process Escalation

### Fast-Track Approval (24-hour turnaround)

- Social media posts (if on-brand guidelines)
- Ad copy variants (if similar to already-approved ads)
- Email broadcasts (if from pre-approved template)

**Process:** Direct approval from channel manager without additional reviews

### Standard Approval (3-5 days)

- Blog posts, landing pages, email campaigns
- Ad creative (if new angle/positioning)
- Video content

**Process:** Editor → Brand → Approver sign-off

### Extended Approval (5-14 days)

- Whitepapers, case studies, major content pieces
- Significant brand repositioning content
- Compliance-sensitive content (healthcare claims, legal disclosures)

**Process:** Full review chain + legal/compliance if needed

### Emergency Override (2-4 hours)

- Time-sensitive news/competitive response
- Founder/leadership directed messaging
- Crisis communication

**Requirements:**
- Approval from Head of GTM + Portfolio Leadership
- Content still reviewed for legal/brand risk
- Post-publication review within 24 hours

## Measurement & Optimization

### Content Performance Tracking

**Blog:**
- Track: Page views, time on page, bounce rate, conversions, shares
- Target: >40% of visitors complete main CTA, >2:00 avg time on page

**Email:**
- Track: Open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate, revenue per email
- Target: >25% open rate, >3% click rate

**Paid Ads:**
- Track: CTR, CPC, conversion rate, ROAS, CAC
- Target: CAC <2x target

**Social:**
- Track: Engagement rate, reach, impression, follower growth
- Target: 2-5% engagement rate (platform-dependent)

### Content Iteration

**Monthly content review:**
1. Identify top 3 performing pieces (by engagement/conversions)
2. Identify bottom 3 performing pieces
3. Analyze why high performers succeeded (topic, format, distribution?)
4. Refresh low performers or sunset if irrelevant

**Quarterly content audit:**
- Remove outdated content
- Update stats/examples in evergreen content
- Identify content gaps (topics customers ask about but not covered)
- Plan new content themes for next quarter

## Legal & Privacy Compliance

### Privacy & Data Protection

- **Email:** Include unsubscribe link in every email
- **Ads:** Disclose targeting data usage in privacy policy
- **Forms:** Privacy notice on any form collecting personal data
- **Case studies:** Get written permission before publishing customer story

### Trademark & Copyright

- **Competitor logos:** Only use if clearly marked as reference/comparison
- **Third-party content:** Always attribute and link to source
- **Images:** Ensure license includes commercial use rights
- **User content:** Get explicit permission before reposting

### Advertising Compliance

- **Healthcare claims:** If health-related product, include appropriate disclaimers
- **Testimonials:** Use real customer testimonials with permission
- **Before/after:** Only use if substantiated and relevant to service
- **Pricing:** Clearly disclose conditions (e.g., annual billing)

### Content Review Frequency

- **Legal review required:** Any new claim, testimonial, pricing messaging, or product feature launch
- **Annual legal audit:** Review all published content for compliance drift

## Editorial Calendar & Planning

### Quarterly Planning

Each quarter, plan:
1. **Content themes:** What will we focus on? (product, thought leadership, customer stories, industry trends)
2. **Content calendar:** Blog, email, ads, social for 90 days
3. **Channel mix:** What % goes to each channel?
4. **Performance targets:** What do we want to achieve?
5. **Resource allocation:** Who creates content, how much time?

### Content Requirements Freeze

**Hard Freeze:** 2 weeks before content publication
- No major changes to content after this point
- Minor edits (grammar, links) still allowed
- Escalate any significant changes to approver

**Rationale:** Allows time for proper review and scheduling

## Content Creator Guidelines

### Every content creator must:
1. Understand brand voice and guidelines
2. Undergo brief (~30 minute) brand training
3. Follow approval workflow (no self-approval)
4. Use content templates where available
5. Document sources for any claims/data
6. Request legal review for compliance-sensitive content

### Content Quality Standards

- **Grammar & spelling:** Zero typos after publication
- **Links:** All links tested before publication
- **Images:** High resolution (no compressed/pixelated images)
- **Formatting:** Proper headings, bullet points, white space
- **Mobile-friendly:** Content readable on mobile devices
