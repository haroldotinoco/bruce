# Discovery Policy

## Scope & Frequency

### Weekly Discovery Cycles
- **Frequency**: Every Monday 9:00 AM UTC
- **Duration**: Target 5-10 minutes for market scanning, 30 minutes for downstream analysis
- **Volume**: 8-25 opportunities discovered per cycle
- **Cycle cadence**: 52 cycles per year with 4 weeks off for portfolio review

### Geographic Scope
- **Primary**: North America (USA, Canada)
- **Secondary**: Western Europe (UK, Germany, France, Nordics)
- **Tertiary**: Singapore, Australia (high-context markets)
- **Emerging**: Rotate quarterly through other high-growth regions

### Industry Verticals
Default coverage rotation to ensure diversity:

**Tier 1 (Always Covered)**
- Healthcare/Life Sciences
- Financial Services/Fintech
- Enterprise Software/SaaS

**Tier 2 (Rotating Quarterly)**
- Manufacturing/Supply Chain
- Sustainability/Climate Tech
- Education/EdTech
- Transportation/Logistics
- Real Estate/PropTech
- AgriTech
- Consumer/DTC

**Tier 3 (Ad-hoc as Signals Emerge)**
- Space/Aerospace
- Biotech
- Energy
- Legal Tech

## Source Selection

### Approved Information Sources

**Primary News/Research**
- TechCrunch, VentureBeat, The Block (industry news)
- Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey reports (market research)
- Industry-specific databases (PitchBook, Crunchbase for funding trends)
- Regulatory agency publications (SEC, CFPB, FDA guidance)

**Emerging Signals**
- LinkedIn hiring trends (filter by company, role, geography)
- Funding announcements (Series A+, indicating problem/market validation)
- Acquisition announcements (market consolidation signals)
- Customer reviews/forums (pain point signals)

**Specialized Sources**
- Industry association reports
- Academic research papers (emerging problems)
- Government statistics (regulatory, economic trends)
- Patent filings (technology enabler signals)

### Excluded Sources
- Social media rumors (Twitter, Reddit speculation without evidence)
- Press releases alone (must corroborate with other sources)
- Unverified startup claims (validate with customer evidence)
- Paid/sponsored content (unless clearly distinguishing signal)

## Problem & Opportunity Criteria

### What Counts as an "Opportunity"
Market opportunity must have ALL of:
1. **Specific Problem**: Measurable, not vague (e.g., "healthcare admin burden" is specific; "healthcare is broken" is not)
2. **Affected Segment**: Identifiable customer group (not "everyone")
3. **Market Signals**: At least 2 independent sources mentioning problem/opportunity
4. **Addressable Market**: Estimated TAM > $10M, < $10B (outside range indicates miscategorization)
5. **Timing**: Signals suggest market is emerging or established (not purely speculative)

### What Does NOT Count

**Reject These Categories**
- Pure speculative future-of-X threads ("AI will revolutionize...")
- Undifferentiated problems solved multiple times already (no new angles)
- Solutions seeking problems (startup is founded, now finding market)
- Overlaps with existing portfolio companies (no direct competition)
- Single-source observations (need corroboration)

## Quality Thresholds

### Discovery Confidence Minimum
- Minimum discovery confidence: **0.6** (60%) to advance to screening
- Below 0.6: Hold for additional research or discard
- Rationale: Confidence < 0.6 indicates opportunity is marginal

### TAM Validation Minimum
- TAM must have minimum **2 data sources** to support estimate
- If TAM cannot be estimated: Reject (cannot evaluate market size tier)
- If TAM is highly speculative: Flag but include with low confidence

### Competitor Requirement
- Minimum 1 competitor/workaround identified
- If zero competitors: Flag as "pre-competitive market" and note accordingly
- If 15+ competitors: Likely saturated, lower priority

## Geographic Prioritization

### Tier 1 Markets (Priority)
- USA (largest TAM, mature market)
- UK (regulatory clarity, similar regulatory environment)
- Germany (manufacturing innovation hub)

### Tier 2 Markets (Secondary)
- Canada, France, Nordics (developed markets)
- Singapore, Australia (high-growth regions)

### Tier 3 Markets (Emerging)
- APAC (except Singapore): India, Indonesia, Philippines (emerging opportunities)
- LATAM: Brazil, Mexico, Colombia (growth markets)
- Middle East: UAE, Saudi Arabia (wealth-driven opportunities)

**Rule**: Opportunities in Tier 1 markets weighted higher than Tier 2. Tier 3 markets only pursued if clear competitive advantage for company.

## Auto-Exclusion Criteria

**Hard Filters** (Automatically Reject)
- Illegal activities (drug markets, weapons, stolen goods)
- Exploitation/safety (child safety, trafficking, abuse)
- High-fraud categories without legitimate use case
- Mature monopolies (unlikely disruption path)
- Requires $500M+ capital raise to get to scale (outside investment capacity)

**Soft Filters** (Flag for Review, Don't Auto-Reject)
- Heavily regulated requiring FDA/pharma approval (flag timeline uncertainty)
- Embargoed countries or sanctioned entities
- Markets with significant IP litigation history
- Cryptocurrency/blockchain use cases (regulatory uncertainty)
- Requires hardware/physical supply chain (capital intensive)

## Prioritization Within Cycle

### Scoring Raw Opportunities
After discovery, rank opportunities within cycle by:
1. **Discovery confidence** (0.6-1.0) — Higher confidence first
2. **TAM tier** (>$1B > $100M-1B > $10-100M) — Larger TAM preferred
3. **Novelty** — First appearance of market vs. known problem
4. **Strategic alignment** — Matches portfolio focus areas

### Volume Management
If discovered opportunities exceed 25 in a cycle:
1. Rank by above criteria
2. Advance top 25 to screening phase
3. Hold remainder for next cycle
4. Never drop discovered opportunities; always hold for future cycles

## Quarterly Review

### Monthly Metrics Review
- Opportunities discovered per cycle (target 15 ± 5)
- Discovery confidence distribution (track % above 0.7)
- Geographic diversity (ensure 3+ geographies represented)
- Vertical diversity (ensure 4+ verticals represented)
- Source mix (ensure no single source > 30% of discoveries)

### Quarterly Deep-Dive
- Market trend analysis (have our focus areas shifted?)
- Competitive landscape review (are new players entering our spaces?)
- Portfolio strategy alignment (are discoveries matching strategic priorities?)
- Process improvement (are policy rules leading to good decisions?)

## Escalation Rules

### When to Escalate to Human Review
1. Opportunity TAM highly uncertain (can't estimate confidently)
2. Regulatory path unclear or controversial (>50% uncertainty on legality/approval)
3. Opportunity conflicts with portfolio values/mission
4. Cluster of opportunities on same theme (need strategic prioritization)
5. Opportunity is directly competitive with existing portfolio company

### When to Decline Processing
- Opportunity submitted without problem statement (incomplete)
- Opportunity is clearly already in portfolio
- Source information is inaccessible/paywalled (cannot validate)
- Opportunity falls under hard-filter auto-exclusion

## Exceptions & Overrides

### When Policy Can Be Overridden
- Portfolio leadership explicitly requests coverage of specific market
- Regulatory environment suddenly changes (new law creates new opportunity)
- Founder/operator directly submits opportunity for evaluation

### Override Documentation
Any override requires:
1. Written explanation of why policy was overridden
2. Approval from portfolio leadership
3. Document in cycle notes for future reference
