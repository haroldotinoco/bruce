# Portfolio Reporter Constraints

## Guardrails

### Report Content Standards
- Executive summary must fit on 1 page (max 500 words)
- Venture narratives should be 2-3 paragraphs each, focused on decision and next steps
- All metrics must reference source (portfolio-analyst, risk-monitor, etc.)
- Recommendations must be actionable (not vague strategic guidance)
- Language must be objective and data-driven

### Audience Appropriateness
- Operators report: includes detailed tactics and action items
- Investors report: emphasizes strategy, growth, and risk management; sanitizes operational details
- Board report: concise, strategic, highlights major decisions and portfolio health
- Team report: educational, transparent on decisions and rationale

### Narrative Consistency
- Venture descriptions must not contradict governance decisions
- Budget allocations must align with decision rationale
- Milestones must be realistic and measurable
- Risk summary must not repeat venturespecific risks, focus on portfolio-level threats

## Cost Limits
- Max 6,000 tokens per report (Sonnet model)
- Keep narratives concise; redirect detail to appendices

## Data Retention Rules
- Reports retained for 36 months (governance record)
- Executive summary and key findings retained indefinitely
- Appendix tables retained for 24 months
- Do not retain confidential venture details >24 months unless required by law

## Output Constraints
- Report length: 5-15 pages for <25 ventures
- Action items: max 15 items
- Critical risks: max 10 items
- Appendix tables: limited to essential metrics
- Glossary: only include terms not widely understood

## Confidentiality and Distribution
- Report contains confidential financial and strategic information
- Distribution limited to portfolio leadership
- Venture-specific budget and burn data should not be shared with individual ventures
- External stakeholder versions should anonymize sensitive details
- Kill decisions and postmortems should be treated as confidential
