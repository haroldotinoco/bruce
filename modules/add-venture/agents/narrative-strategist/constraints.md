# Narrative-Strategist Constraints

## Content Requirements

### Mandatory Sections
1. Brand Narrative (hero's journey): 300-400 words
2. Positioning & Messaging: 200-300 words with 3-5 pillars
3. Stakeholder-Specific Narratives: 200-300 words (4 audiences)
4. Messaging Framework: 200-300 words with proof points
5. Launch & Communication Strategy: 150-200 words

**Total target: 1,000-1,500 words**

### Quality Standards
- Narrative grounded in Vol 1 opportunity thesis
- Positioning delivers on Vol 3 differentiation promises
- Messaging pillars resonate with Vol 2 ICP
- Tone is consistent and distinct
- Stakeholder narratives appropriately differentiated

## Narrative Standards

### Brand Narrative Requirements
- Hero's journey structure: Problem → Hero → Challenge → Transformation → Resolution
- Problem is customer's problem (not ours)
- Hero is customer succeeding (not us)
- Transformation is specific outcome
- Resolution is sustained success
- Macro context supports "why now"

### Messaging Pillars
- Minimum 3, maximum 5 pillars
- Each pillar must have supporting evidence
- Pillars must be distinct (not overlapping)
- At least one pillar must differentiate vs. competitors
- Language must be customer-focused (not feature-focused)

## Validation Standards

### Assumptions Documentation
- Minimum 3 narrative assumptions
- Examples: "Customers prioritize trust over novelty", "Investors value market timing", "Brand personality should feel expert, not flashy"
- Each assumption must have validation approach

### Data Gaps Handling
- Flag messaging gaps (brand personality not yet validated)
- Propose testing (customer messaging interviews, investor feedback)
- Adjust confidence if critical narrative elements untested

## Execution Constraints

### Time Limits
- Target execution: 120 seconds
- Absolute timeout: 5 minutes

### Cost Management
- Anthropic Claude Opus: ~0.10 per volume
- Maximum cost: 0.20 per volume

## Confidence Score Requirements

### Scoring Guidance
- **80-100**: Clear brand narrative, positioning is distinct, messaging resonates, stakeholder narratives differ appropriately
- **60-79**: Reasonable narrative, positioning logical, messaging adequate, some stakeholder nuance missing
- **40-59**: Narrative unclear or generic, positioning not clearly differentiated, messaging weak
- **Below 40**: Narrative doesn't engage, positioning indistinct, messaging confusing

## Integration Constraints

### Upstream Dependencies
- Vol 1 opportunity thesis (informs "why now" and brand purpose)
- Vol 3 value proposition (messaging must deliver on differentiation)
- Vol 5 GTM positioning (narrative must support GTM positioning)

### Downstream Dependencies
- No downstream agents depend on narrative
- Narrative used by all customer-facing communications

## Quality Assurance

### Validation Checklist
- [ ] All 5 sections populated
- [ ] Total word count 1,000-1,500
- [ ] Brand narrative follows hero's journey structure
- [ ] Customer is the hero (not company)
- [ ] One-liner is memorable and distinct
- [ ] Elevator pitches are at appropriate lengths
- [ ] Messaging pillars are distinct (not overlapping)
- [ ] Tone of voice is consistent and describable
- [ ] Investor hook is compelling and specific
- [ ] Stakeholder narratives are appropriately differentiated
- [ ] Proof points are credible and specific
- [ ] All claims grounded in Vols 1, 3, 5
- [ ] Key assumptions listed (minimum 3)
- [ ] Data gaps explicitly acknowledged
- [ ] Confidence score justified with rationale

## Error Handling

### Graceful Degradation
- If brand narrative unclear: provide simpler structure, adjust confidence down
- If positioning not distinct: flag competitive similarity, recommend differentiation focus
- If stakeholder narratives too similar: flag and recommend greater differentiation
