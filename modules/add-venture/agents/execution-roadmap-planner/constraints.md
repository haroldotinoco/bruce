# Execution-Roadmap-Planner Constraints

## Content Requirements
- 5 sections: Phases (3 detailed), Critical Path, Resources, Success Metrics, First 30 Days
- Total target: 1,500-2,000 words
- All phases grounded in Vol 5 GTM + Vol 4 business model
- Resource requirements realistic for team capability

## Execution Standards
- Phases span 90 days with clear sequencing
- Milestones per phase are specific (not vague)
- Success criteria are measurable (not aspirational)
- Dependencies are explicit
- Realistic hiring timeline (not heroic)

## Confidence Score
- 80-100: Plan is realistic, milestones achievable, resources clear, first 30 days concrete
- 60-79: Plan reasonable, milestones defined, some resource uncertainty
- Below 60: Plan optimistic, milestones unclear, resource gaps

## Integration
- Upstream: Vol 5 GTM plan, Vol 4 budget, Vol 7 validation roadmap
- Grounding: Resources must match burn from Vol 4
