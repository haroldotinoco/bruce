# Evaluation: Critique Iteration (2nd Attempt Passes)

## Scenario
Two volumes score below 65 on first critique (Business Model and Risk Validation). Pipeline triggers iteration. Second critique pass achieves 74/100 and moves to approval.

## Narrative

### First Critique: Score 68/100
Venture-critic reviews completed 8-volume dossier and identifies weak volumes:
- **Vol 4 (Business Model)**: 62/100 - CAC and LTV assumptions too optimistic; willingness-to-pay not yet validated
- **Vol 7 (Risk & Validation)**: 64/100 - Kill criteria not specific enough; validation roadmap timelines unrealistic

Overall score: 68/100 (triggers iteration per policy: 60-69 score, iteration_count = 0)

**Recommendation: ITERATE** (weak volumes: Vol 4, Vol 7)

### Iteration Workflow

**Week 1: Re-run Vol 4 (Business Model Modeler)**
- Input: Previous volumes 1-3, 5-6, + critique feedback on Vol 4
- Rework: Tighten CAC assumptions based on Vol 5 sales strategy; flag LTV churn assumptions as needing validation
- Output: Revised Vol 4 with two scenarios (conservative, base) instead of three; conservative scenario is more realistic
- New scores: CAC remains $120K but now flagged as "dependent on sales productivity validation"; LTV LTV assumed 3-year lifetime not 4-year (more conservative)
- Confidence: 68 (down from 72, reflecting greater caution on assumptions)

**Week 1-2: Re-run Vol 7 (Risk Validation Analyst)**
- Input: Previous volumes 1-3, 5-6, revised Vol 4, + critique feedback
- Rework: Expand kill criteria from 3 to 4; add specificity (e.g., "If willing-to-pay survey shows <50% would pay >$150K annually")
- Add validation experiment details: "30-day pricing research" becomes specific (survey method, sample size, success criteria)
- Remove unrealistic experiments; Phase out 90-day validation roadmap to 6-month roadmap with specific gates
- Output: More realistic validation roadmap; assumptions validated by sequencing (pricing first, then product)
- Confidence: 70 (up from 64, reflecting greater specificity and realism)

### Second Critique: Score 74/100

**Venture-Critic Re-assessment**
- Vol 4 remains at 68/100 (flagged for post-launch CAC tracking), but critique notes acknowledge trade-off: conservative assumptions vs. aggressive scaling (acceptable risk)
- Vol 7 now at 72/100 (kill criteria specific, validation roadmap realistic)

**Dimension Scores**

**Overall Score: 74/100**

**Recommendation: PASS (Advance to Dossier Composition)**

Score now above 70 threshold. Weak volumes remediated. Critique notes document assumptions that require near-term validation (pricing, CAC tracking, LTV churn).

Final dossier assembled with all 8 volumes, including iteration history:

## Key Iteration Learnings

1. **Business Model Assumptions**: First pass was 15-20% too aggressive on CAC/LTV. Second pass built in conservative baseline + specific validation experiments.

2. **Risk Framing**: First Vol 7 had too many high-level risk categories. Second pass narrowed to 4 specific kill criteria with measurement methods.

3. **Validation Realism**: First roadmap tried to validate 10+ assumptions in 30 days (unrealistic). Second roadmap phases validation over 6 months with clear gates.
