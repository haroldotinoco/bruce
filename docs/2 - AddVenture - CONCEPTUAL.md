# Addventure — Conceptual documentation

## 1. Vision

Addventure is not a “business plan generator”. It is a **business formulation engine** — an AI system that reproduces the rigor of good strategy consulting, venture studio, or early-stage advisor: from opportunity diagnosis to risk mapping to execution roadmap.

The core belief is simple:

> “Not to generate a nice PDF from nothing. To produce a complete, auditable, justified, iterable, and exportable **decision system**.”

## 2. Problem

Most “business idea” tools fail because:

- They deliver **filled templates** (Business Model Canvas, Lean Canvas) with no intelligence between blocks.
- They treat strategy as **one prompt → one document**, with no sequencing between stages.
- They confuse text volume with clarity: lots of boilerplate, little thesis.
- They don't connect **offering, market, monetization, and go-to-market** in a coherent narrative.
- They don't expose **falsifiable hypotheses** or risks — just “encourage ideas”.

The result is always the same: **output that looks generic or spreadsheet-like**, not investor or founder decision.

## 3. Core philosophy

### 3.1 Process over document

Serious consulting firms follow a sequence: opportunity diagnosis → customer/market architecture → value proposition → business model → GTM → positioning → validation → roadmap. Addventure mirrors this discipline. Each module feeds the next; stages are not skipped for speed.

### 3.2 Justification over filling

Every relevant assertion — segment, pricing, channel, wedge — must carry a rationale:

- **Why this ICP and not another?** (behavior, urgency, alternatives)
- **Why this revenue model?** (unit economics, defensibility, analogies)
- **Where can the thesis fail?** (honest limitations)

Assertions without “why” are what make output generic. Assertions anchored in hypothesis and evidence are what make output strategic.

### 3.3 Exploration before consolidation

The system separates modes:

| Mode | Purpose | Output |
| ---- | --------- | ----- |
| **Divergent exploration** | Open up space for models and angles | Multiple monetization scenarios, GTM routes, positioning framings |
| **Convergent consolidation** | Choose and refine with criteria | One core business line, compared and prioritized |

Mixing both phases is the most common cause of “beautiful but useless” plans.

### 3.4 AI proposes, system structures and audits

AI is the reasoning and synthesis engine. **Not** a black box that substitutes human judgment:

- **AI is strong at**: analysis, structuring, assisted research, scenarios, critique, narrative.
- **The product is strong at**: JSON schemas, stage-by-stage pipeline, approval trail, consistent export, risk and hypothesis mapping.

The final deliverable must be **actionable and traceable**, not just long text.

### 3.5 Critique as a resource

A critique agent (or stage) evaluates proposals with hard questions:

- Is the pain real or invented?
- Is the “must-have” clear?
- Is the business model coherent with the channel and the ICP?
- Which hypotheses, if false, would undermine the thesis?

Self-critique is not optional — it is the mechanism that raises the standard and reduces self-deception.

## 4. Business as a decision system, not a single artifact

Addventure does not deliver “a canvas”. It delivers a **venture decision package**, organized in volumes aligned to *Layer 0 — Venture / Business Strategy*:

### Volume 1 — Opportunity diagnosis

Core problem, urgency, context, alternatives, failures of current solutions, market signals, opportunity hypothesis.

### Volume 2 — Customer and market architecture

ICP, buyer and user personas, jobs-to-be-done, objections, market dynamics, competitive benchmark.

### Volume 3 — Value proposition system

Core proposition, promised transformation, perceived differential, benefits, proof, and perceived risk.

### Volume 4 — Business model design

Segments, channels, relationships, key activities and resources, partners, costs, revenues, retention, growth loops — with **comparable scenarios** (SaaS, marketplace, service, etc.), not a single rigid canvas.

### Volume 5 — Go-to-market blueprint

Wedge, initial channel, entry offering, acquisition, onboarding, activation, retention, expansion, initial traction plan.

### Volume 6 — Positioning and narrative

Category, competitive frame, positioning, thesis, core message, one-liner, pitch — natural bridge to brand identity (sister product or next phase), without confusing business with visual branding.

### Volume 7 — Validation and risk map

Critical hypotheses, adoption risks, pricing, operations and competition, falsification signals, what to validate before investing heavily.

### Volume 8 — Execution roadmap

Recommended MVP, v1 scope, what not to build now, 30/60/90-day milestones, initial metrics, priorities.

This is what makes the output look like consulting work — not just template filling.

## 5. Design principles

| Principle | Meaning |
| --------- | ----------- |
| **Depth over speed** | A complete process beats a quick and shallow plan. |
| **Structure over improvisation** | JSON schemas, structured outputs, and rigid pipeline avoid useless loose text. |
| **Thesis over checklist** | A defensible business beats a list of buzzwords. |
| **Transparency over “magic”** | Each block is traceable, auditable, and explainable. |
| **System over single PDF** | The deliverable is the decision architecture, not a single nice page. |
| **Critique over praise** | The system should try to refute its own thesis before packaging. |

## 6. What Addventure is not

- Not a **generic business plan generator** in a single shot.
- Not a **canvas filler** without coherence between blocks.
- Not a tool that **replaces real-world validation** (interviews, sales, experiments).
- Not **visual identity** or brand manual — that is another product or phase; here only the narrative bridge (Volume 6) aligns with branding.

## 7. Aspiration

Addventure seeks to be the intersection of:

- **Language models** as strategic brain and synthesizer.
- **Multi-agent orchestration** as a source of depth and non-genericness.
- **Research and data** (when available) as market anchoring.
- **Risk and hypothesis map** as “anti-delusion” credibility.
- **Optional: human in the loop** at review and approval gates (in the current product: briefing, business model scenario selection, and critique before dossier — see `docs/FUNCTIONAL.md` and `docs/TECHNICAL.md`).

The product vision is a **premium business architecture engine** — not “AI-generated text”, but AI that conducts a venture process comparable to that of good advisors and consulting firms.
